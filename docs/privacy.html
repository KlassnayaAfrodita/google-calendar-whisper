Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Overview

Google Calendar Whisper is a Telegram bot that helps users manage Google Calendar events. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app accesses and how that data is used.

Data We Access

The app may access or store the following data:

  • Telegram chat ID and Telegram username, when available.
  • Google OAuth tokens needed to connect to Google Calendar.
  • Google Calendar event data needed to answer user commands, including event titles, times, dates, locations, descriptions, and event IDs.
  • User messages sent to the Telegram bot for calendar-related commands.

Google Calendar Permission

The app uses https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events so users can create, view, update, and delete their own Google Calendar events through Telegram commands.

The app does not use Google Calendar data for advertising, does not sell Google user data, and does not share Google Calendar data with third parties except as needed to provide the requested bot functionality.

How We Use Data

  • To authenticate the user's Google Calendar connection.
  • To create, read, update, or delete calendar events requested by the user.
  • To show schedules and send reminder messages requested or enabled by the user.
  • To operate, debug, and secure the service.

Data Retention And Revocation

OAuth tokens are stored while the user remains connected to the bot. A user can revoke the app's access at any time from their Google Account permissions page. After access is revoked, the bot will no longer be able to access Google Calendar for that user.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the app owner at the support email listed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
