Google Calendar Whisper

A Telegram bot that helps users manage Google Calendar events with text and voice commands.

What The App Does

Google Calendar Whisper lets a user connect their Google Calendar account and then use Telegram messages to create, view, update, and delete calendar events.

  • Create events from natural language messages.
  • Update or delete events selected by the user.
  • Show today's schedule and upcoming events.
  • Send daily calendar reminders when enabled.

Google Calendar Access

The app requests the Google Calendar Events permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events. This permission is used only to work with the user's calendar events.

The app does not request access to calendar sharing settings, calendar ACLs, or calendar settings.

Support

For support or data access questions, contact the app owner at the support email listed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
